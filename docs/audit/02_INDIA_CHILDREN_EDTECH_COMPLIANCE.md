## Executive Compliance Verdict

AcademicLevel explicitly serves K–12 students in India (Classes 1–12, legal minors under 18). At baseline commit `5604856`, the platform has **CRITICAL non-compliance gaps** under **DPDP Act 2023 Section 9** (and **DPDP Rules 2025 Rule 10**), **CERT-In Directions (2022)**, **CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines (2023)**, **IT Rules (2021)**, and **child-safety / NEP 2020** norms — directly contradicting public marketing claims (`AboutPage.tsx:41` *"Compliant with the DPDP Act 2023"*).
